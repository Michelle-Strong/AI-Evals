## Method + Ground Truth (two ✅/⚠️ cells)

### Hallucination Rate
- **Method:** LLM-as-Judge against the gold dataset of 500 email outputs.
- **Ground truth:** Pass = LLM-as -Judge returns Pass on >99% of gold dataset.

### Bias (Fairness)
- **Method:** Human evaluations across 50 emails from 50 different users.
- **Ground truth:** Pass = >95% quality consistency across users.

## Strategic acceptance

**Accepted gap:** Latency

Latency isn't a safety, fairness, or legal-exposure dimension for an executive summaries tool. Willing to accept this risk as Latency is anticipated to be low impact for users obtaining outputs to research material.

## Critical mitigation

**Critical gap:** Toxicity (UX Trust)

**Why critical:** we accept this as a gap because we do not yet have a validated instrument to measure it, and we are relying on Hallucination/Bias/Toxicity coverage as a partial proxy — acknowledging this proxy does not cover disclosure, confidence-signalling, or over-reliance risk.

**Mitigation plan:** Deploy an automated toxicity classifier as a pre-release gate on 100% of outputs, backed by a 300-item gold dataset stratified across explicit toxicity, subtle bias-coded language, and adversarial source-document injection — pass threshold ≥98%.
