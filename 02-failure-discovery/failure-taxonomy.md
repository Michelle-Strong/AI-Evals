## Defending the Prioritization

- The #1 risk is rated **P0**, severity over frequency. Even rare catastrophic failures block the launch when the business consequence is contract liability or churn at the Enterprise tier.

- Severity is the qualitative business judgment, anchored to the user promise defined in the Module 1 Strategy Canvas.
